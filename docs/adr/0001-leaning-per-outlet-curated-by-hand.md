# Leaning is assigned per Outlet, by hand, and Outlets are not balanced

Every Article inherits the Leaning of its Outlet. That Leaning is set by hand in config. Each Outlet's Leaning is backed by cited public sources, which are published on the site. AI never classifies Articles as left/centre/right. Rating each Article with AI would be subtle, easy to dispute and opaque. A per-Outlet rating that people can check is cheap, stable and easy to defend (it's also what Ground News does).

Outlets are chosen for readership or editorial significance. We don't pick them to balance the Leanings. So when the French press landscape is uneven, Coverage shows it on purpose: that's part of what Prisme reveals.

## Consequences

- An Opinion piece and a news Article from the same Outlet share one Leaning, even when their tone differs.
- Changing an Outlet's Leaning changes the Coverage of past Editions too, unless each Edition records the Leaning in force when it was built.
