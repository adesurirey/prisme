# Stories are grouped incrementally, and frozen Story files are permanent

Editions rebuild hourly, all 24 hours (ADR-0002), with no database, and a Story page's URL must live forever. Each build therefore starts from the existing Story files and only assigns Articles that aren't in a Story yet; membership never changes once assigned. An Article less than 24 hours old keeps its Story live; a Story with no live Articles left leaves the Edition, and its file and page stay published, frozen — never modified, never reopened. Amended in ADR-0017: a Story belongs to one Publication day and stays open to ingestion only until 06:00 Europe/Paris the day after its day — liveness is bounded by the daily cycle, and an Article may only join a Story of its own Publication day. A later Article about the same event starts a new Story. New Articles are grouped by the Grouping model (Gemini Flash-Lite, current generation, direct from Google's free tier: one structured-output call per build, with a ~45 s backoff before its single retry — per-minute quotas reset on that timescale); every merge into an existing Story gets a Jev yes/no Membership check — the model's own confidence never bypasses it, because a generic Story title makes it easy to be confidently wrong — and a failed Grouping call (HTTP, unparseable after retries, or a missing key) fails the build: nothing is written, the last committed Edition stays live, CI goes red. Amended in issue #40: the original policy — degrade to one-Article Stories rather than fail — was reversed after the 2026-10-08 builds, where one Gemini 429 wrote 456 permanent singletons in a shot and a full wipe-and-rebuild (PR #38) was the only fix. Membership and Classification fail the build only when every check fails (the model is down): isolated failures keep splitting and keep Articles unclassified, retried next build. Summaries still degrade — cosmetic, a later build picks the Story up. The seed of a proposed new Story is checked too (issue #36): across builds against the most headline-similar live Stories, and within the build against the other surviving seeds, so no path creates a Story without a Membership check; a yes folds the proposed Story into the checked Story. All Membership checks — seeds and merges alike — share the ≤ 20 per-build budget, guard checks first: starving a merge splits (self-heals next build), an unguarded seed creates a duplicate Story.

## Considered Options

- Regenerate all groupings from scratch each build: would reshuffle ids and slugs, which must be permanent.
- Reopen frozen Stories when a fresh Article matches: editing a "permanent" file defeats permanence; a new Story across a gap keeps the rule simple.
- Keep an assignments cache alongside the Story files: redundant — the files are the record (ADR-0002).
- Retro-merge the tracer bullet's one-Article Story files: rewriting "permanent" files. They were deleted once instead, before this shipped.
- Route Gemini through OpenRouter like the Decision model: paid per token, while the free tier (~1,000 requests/day) dwarfs the ~12 calls a day the build cadence needs.

## Consequences

- A wrong merge or wrong split is permanent. The Membership check (every merge into an existing Story, ≤ 20 per build, refuse or fail → split) and the fail-to-split bias limit the damage, bounded by the build-fail rule: a run where every check failed writes nothing. There is no offline benchmark for grouping; each PR gets a spot-check on a real day (issue #5). The tracer-bullet build created a thematic catch-all Story (« Perspectives économiques et financières de la France ») that swallowed a distinct affair: the prompt now bans theme-level Stories, and confidence no longer skips the check (the file was deleted; a fresh build re-grouped its Articles).
- Titles are written by the dedicated Title pass (ADR-0023), never by the Grouping model. A Story's title is set at birth — the slug derives from it and is fixed at creation: across builds the existing file keeps its slug, within a build the smallest Story id keeps the bare slug, and other collisions get a Story-id suffix — resolved deterministically from ids, never by ordering. A live Story that gained Articles may be re-titled by the pass when a better title exists; a retitle never re-derives the slug, and a frozen Story's title is immutable like its slug. (Amended in ADR-0023: previously the Grouping model itself could re-propose titles on every build.)
- The old split path is gone (amended in issue #40, fail-not-degrade); this consequence's founding-headline rule is superseded by the Title pass: every Story is titled at birth by the pass, which fails the build rather than write an untitled Story.
- A failed Grouping call loses the Articles collected during that build (feeds keep rolling, ~1 h of tail). Accepted in issue #40: losing the tail is cheap next to permanent singletons, and CI red on quota spikes is the visible signal the degrade policy lacked.
- Amended in issue #75: the file and page stay published forever only for Stories that entered the Edition at least once (`everInEdition`, sticky, set while the Story is live). A frozen Story that never ranked into the Edition is deleted at the end of each run — nothing links to it (the homepage links only Edition Stories) and there is no sitemap, so no published URL breaks. Once-in-Edition Story files remain permanent; a deleted Story's Articles simply start a fresh Story if the event resurfaces.
- Google's free tier may train on prompts: only public RSS headlines and teasers are ever sent (same posture as ADR-0003).
